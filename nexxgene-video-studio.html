04Compliance guardrails

  • Both are "Health Supplement — not for medical use." Never say cure / treat / detox-as-medicine / melts / burns fat.
  • No timelines or guarantees ("lose 4kg in 4 weeks"). Fast&Up gambles on these; your 64-day lesson says the dose angle is what actually lasts.
  • Super Slim creative needs 18+ targeting (any weight framing triggers it). Frame as metabolism, energy, fullness, less bloating — not fat-loss promises.
  • Don't use the FSSAI licence as a promo badge (FSSAI prohibits it). Safe trust lines: "US FDA-registered facility," "every dose declared," "0g sugar."
  • Testimonials carry "results vary." No pinched-fat / body-shaming visuals.
  • Safe verbs: supports, helps, formulated for, visibly, feel. Glow skin claims are safer than Slim weight claims — keep Slim on energy/metabolism.

08Economics & conversion (updated for real prices)

Your margins are excellent — this is good news for ads. At COGS ~₹13/sachet (Glow) and ~₹11 (Slim), a 14-sachet pack costs roughly ₹182 / ₹154 to make against ₹999 / ₹899 MRP — about 81–83% gross margin. Naive break-even is ~1.2x ROAS; after shipping, COD and RTO, realistic break-even sits around 1.5–1.7x. A new nutra brand lands 1.4–2.1x in month 1, so you can be profitable early — if the site converts.
  • Hook in 3 seconds, number or pain first, never the logo. Ship 5+ hooks per concept — the hook is 80% of performance.
  • Caption everything (85% watch muted) — each VO line doubles as burned-in text.
  • Match ad → landing page. An "every dose declared" ad must land on a page that opens with the declared panel. (Your hero page needs its prices/claims corrected first — see note below.)
  • Seed 30–50 reviews before scaling. Bigger conversion lever than any ad tweak; social-proofed supplement sites convert ~6.8% vs ~1.5%.
  • Offer ladder: cold → single pack + free shipping; retarget → The Stack (Glow + Slim) bundle; festivals → BOGO (what The Body Love wins with).
  • Test matrix: 3 concepts × 3 hooks = 9 ads in one ad set, ₹1,500–2,000/day, judge after 4–5 days. At your budget, consolidate into one ad set so it can approach Meta's 50-events/week learning threshold.
