02The 5 AI-video formats that are working

What the category is buying traffic on — and what AI video (Veo / Flow / Sora) can now produce cheaply.

1. The Dose/Absorption Exposé

Miduty, Chicnutrix, the "1500mg vs 500mg" ad

Number-on-screen, myth-busting, "they hide this" energy. AI makes this easy: molecular viz of peptides passing (or failing to pass) through the gut wall.

2. UGC Talking-Head Testimonial

Everyone — the workhorse format

Real-feeling person: problem → discovery → result. AI avatars now do this; or film one real creator and cut 10 hooks.

3. ASMR Ritual / "Glow in every sip"

Plix, Niconi, The Wellness Shop

Satisfying macro: sachet tear, pour, dissolve, first sip. AI excels at hyper-real macro liquid + product shots.

4. Problem-Agitate VSL

Patrick Walsh (US), Ayurvedic pages

"If you've been told X…" — names a daily pain (3 PM crash, dull skin), agitates, resolves. Longer, great for cold retargeting.

5. Offer / Festive Urgency

The Body Love, Beautywise (BOGO, SHARK20)

Bundle, BOGO, countdown. Lowest creative lift, best for bottom-funnel & retargeting — not for cold prospecting.

Your plan: lead cold traffic with #1 (Absorption Exposé) and #2 (UGC); use #3 (ASMR) for thumb-stopping reach; retarget with #4 (VSL) and #5 (Offer).

04Compliance guardrails (read before you write a word)

This protects your ad account and your brand from ₹10L FSSAI penalties. Fast&Up and Beautywise run "Lose 4 kilos in 4 weeks" / "Shed inches in 12 weeks" — timeline + weight-loss promises. They're gambling; don't copy them.
  • No timeline or guaranteed outcomes ("lose X kg in Y days", "guaranteed glow"). Meta rejects these and FSSAI penalises them.
  • No disease claims (cure, treat, detox-as-medicine). Marine-collagen skin claims are far safer than weight-loss claims.
  • Super Slim creative needs 18+ targeting — any fat/weight framing triggers it. Frame Slim as metabolic energy / feeling lighter / beating the slump, not fat-loss promises.
  • Don't use the FSSAI logo/licence as a promo badge — FSSAI prohibits it. "Third-party Eurofins tested" is your safe trust line.
  • Testimonials need "results vary" framing. Avoid pinched-fat / body-shaming visuals entirely.
  • Safe verbs: supports, helps, formulated for, visibly, feel. Banned verbs: cures, guarantees, melts, burns fat, detoxes.

07Turning views into buyers

  • Hook in 3 seconds or lose them. Indian mobile feeds bounce in 4–5s. Lead with the number or the pain, never the logo. Make 5+ hook variants per concept — the hook is 80% of performance.
  • Caption the first line as on-screen text — 85% watch on mute. Every VO line above doubles as burned-in text.
  • Match the ad to the landing page. An "absorption" ad must hit a page that opens with absorption — your new light hero does this. Don't send a <2,000 Da ad to a generic homepage.
  • Seed 30–50 reviews before you scale spend. Per the India D2C playbook, zero reviews is a bigger conversion leak than any ad tweak. Supplement sites with strong social proof convert ~6.8% vs ~1.5% baseline.
  • Offer structure: cold traffic → single SKU + free shipping over ₹1,499; retargeting → the 24H bundle (Save ₹399) + BOGO on festivals (what The Body Love is winning with).
  • COD + 90-day guarantee stated on the ad end-card — removes the two biggest Indian purchase frictions.
  • Testing matrix: run 3 concepts × 3 hooks each (9 ads) in one ad set, ₹1,500–2,000/day, let it run 4–5 days before judging. Kill the bottom, reshoot hooks on the winner.
Reality check on spend (from the India D2C playbook): a new nutra brand realistically sees 1.4–2.1x ROAS in month 1, climbing to 2.8–3.6x by month 6 once reviews exist. At ₹30–60k/month you won't clear Meta's 50-conversions/week learning threshold, so consolidate into one campaign / one ad set and optimise for Add-to-Cart early. Anyone promising 4x+ out of the gate is selling, not forecasting.

08Production workflow — from prompt to posted ad

  1. Lock the product reference. Generate one clean jar + one canister still (or shoot real ones). Save them — they're the image reference for every clip.
  2. Generate stills in ChatGPT / DALL·E using the IMAGE prompts. Pick the best frame per scene.
  3. Animate in Google Flow / Veo using the VIDEO prompts; feed your product still as the reference image so packaging stays consistent. Generate 6–8s clips.
  4. For talking-head UGC: either film one real creator (best trust) or use an AI avatar tool; keep the same person across hook variants.
  5. Assemble in CapCut / Premiere: stack hook → science/story → product → CTA. Burn in the on-screen text. Add licensed music + SFX.
  6. Caption everything (mute-first). Export 9:16 for Reels/Stories, plus a 1:1 and 4:5 variant for feed.
  7. Ship 9 ads (3 concepts × 3 hooks) into one ad set and let the data pick the winner.
